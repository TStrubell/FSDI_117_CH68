About Me

I’m Tim Strubell, a medically retired U.S. Army veteran with 23 years of service, including 18 years in Special Operations. My career taught me discipline, readiness, and how to lead when the stakes are high.

After retiring, I founded Strubell Publishing LLC and expanded into Strubell Goods & Gear (SGnG), a brand focused on rugged, purpose‑built equipment and systems inspired by real‑world experience.

I’m currently a Full Stack Development Immersive student at San Diego Global Knowledge University, working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and Django to build clean, unified web systems. I care deeply about structure—whether in code, documentation, or physical workshop plans.

My goal is simple: build things that are clear, durable, and genuinely useful—whether that’s a portfolio, a workshop, or a gear concept under the SGnG banner.
